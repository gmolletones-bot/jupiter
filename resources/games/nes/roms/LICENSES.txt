NES homebrew games bundled with Jupiter
=======================================

These are new games written for the NES by independent developers. None of
them is a Nintendo game, and none is licensed or endorsed by Nintendo.

Lawn Mower (lawn-mower.nes)
  By Shiru, 2011. "It is free of charge, released into Public Domain."
  (from the game's manual)

LAN Master (lan-master.nes)
  By Shiru, 2011. "It is free of charge, released into Public Domain."
  (from the game's manual)

Chase (chase.nes)
  By Shiru, 2012. "Feel free to do anything you want with this code,
  consider it Public Domain." (from the game's source, game.c)

Zooming Secretary (zooming-secretary.nes)
  By Alexei Bespalko (PinWizz) and Shiru, 2011.
  Released under the Creative Commons Attribution license (CC BY).
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Concentration Room (concentration-room.nes)
  Copyright Damian Yerrick. Free software under the GNU General Public
  License, version 3 or later, with this exception: "you may copy and
  distribute exact copies of the program, as published by Damian Yerrick,
  in iNES or UNIF executable form without source code."
  Source: https://github.com/pinobatch/croom-nes

Shiru's games: https://shiru.untergrund.net/software.shtml
